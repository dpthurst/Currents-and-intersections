In recent work of Cantrell and Oreg\'on-Reyes and, independently, Kapovich and the first author, the following property has played a key role in extending stable lengths coming from group actions of hyperbolic groups $G$ acting on uniformly quasi-geodesic hyperbolic spaces $X$.
Roughly, a Gromov hyperbolic space is a metric space whose triangles are thin. A group $G$ is said to be Gromov hyperbolic if its Cayley graph is.
A metric space $X$ is uniformy quasi-geodesic if there exists constants $\lambda_1,\lambda_2 >0$ and $\epsilon \geq 0$, so that every two points $x, y \in X$ are connected by a $(\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\epsilon)$-quasi-geodesic.
We say that $G \acts X$ of a non-elementary hyperbolic group on a uniformly quasi-geodesic hyperbolic metric space $X$ has the \emph{bounded backtracking property} if given an orbit map $f \colon \Cay(G) \to X$, there exist a constant $C>0$ so that for every two points $x,y \in \Cay(G)$, and geodesic $[x,y]$, there exists some quasi-geodesic $[f(x),f(y)]$ in $X$ so that $f([x,y]) \subset N_C([f(x),f(y)])$, where $N_C([f(x),f(y)])$ denotes the $C$-neighborhood of $[f(x),f(y)])$ with respect to the Hausdorff metric.

\begin{question}
Let $G$ be a surface group, and $G \acts X$ an action on a hyperbolic quasi-geodesic metric space $X$.
Let $\ell_X$ denote the stable length of the action.
Suppose that $\ell_X \in \QConvex(S)$. Does it follow that $G \acts X$ has the bounded backtracking property?
\end{question}

\begin{question}
Let $G$ be a surface group, and $G \acts X$ an action on a hyperbolic quasi-geodesic metric space $X$ with bounded backtracking property.
Let $\ell_X$ denote the stable length of the action. Does it follow that $\ell_X \in \QConvex(S)$.
\end{question}
