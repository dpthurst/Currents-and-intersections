\title{Currents and intersections}
\author[Martínez-Granado]{Dídac Martínez-Granado}
\address{Department of Mathematics\\University of Luxembourg\\Av. de la Fonte 6, Esch-sur-Alzette, L-4364, Luxembourg}       
\email{didac.martinezgranado@uni.lu}

\author[Thurston]{Dylan~P.~Thurston}
\address{Department of Mathematics\\
         Indiana University,
         Bloomington, Indiana 47405\\
         USA}
\email{dpthurst@indiana.edu}
\date{Draft of \today}
\begin{abstract}
We give a new definition of geodesic currents in terms of functions on
curves satisfying the ``smoothing property'', i.e., non-increasing
under surgery of essential crossings of curves.
As applications, we give new characterizations of measured laminations and hyperbolic metric lengths as curve functionals, a new proof of Skora's theorem and show that cross-ratio periods satisfy the hypotheses of our theorem and thus induce geodesic currents.
\end{abstract}
